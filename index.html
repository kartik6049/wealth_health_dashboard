    Data

    Gapminder five-year dataset via Plotly's public datasets repository: 142 countries, 1952–2007. Values between snapshots are interpolated for smooth animation.

    Method

    Continent, world and KPI averages are population-weighted. GDP per capita is inflation-adjusted US dollars on a log scale.

    Built with

    D3.js scales on HTML Canvas, CanvasJS trend lines and Google Charts GeoChart. Made by Kartik.
